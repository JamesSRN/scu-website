About SCU

The Saturday Clinic for the Uninsured (SCU) is a student-run free clinic providing comprehensive care for uninsured patients in Milwaukee. We promote health and wellness in our community while training the next generation of health professionals.

Current Services

Primary Care

Management of conditions, including:

  • High blood pressure
  • Diabetes
  • Infections
  • Vision
  • High cholesterol
  • Asthma
  • Rashes, joint/back pain
  • Mental health
  • Basic women’s health (mammograms)
  • STI testing, including HIV
  • Routine health visits, work physicals, and screenings
  • Free or discounted prescription medications and labs (performed at Ascension Columbia St. Mary’s Hospital)
  • Social work consults
  • Health education
  • Virtual and in-person translators
  • Free or discounted specialty clinics (referral needed)
